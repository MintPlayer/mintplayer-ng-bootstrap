Rating

A star rating as native radios in a radiogroup, with two-way binding via [(value)] (0 for none) and a configurable [maximum]. Arrow keys move between the stars, the value submits with a form under name, and it works without JavaScript. (previewchange) reports the star under the pointer (null when it leaves); label and starLabel localise the names.

Basic usage

{{ previewValue() }}

Angular forms

Template-driven [(ngModel)] and reactive [formControl] both bind through the component's ControlValueAccessor: the value, the disabled state, and touched when focus leaves.
