Toast

Toasts are transient, dismissible notifications. A ToastQueue from @mintplayer/ui-core/toast holds them: push(data) queues one and close(id) dismisses it. <bs-toaster [queue]> renders the queue in a fixed corner, with its bsToasterToast template once per entry; compose <bs-toast> with <bs-toast-header> and <bs-toast-body> there. A toast announces its text when it shows, and hides itself after five seconds unless the pointer or focus is on the toasts.
