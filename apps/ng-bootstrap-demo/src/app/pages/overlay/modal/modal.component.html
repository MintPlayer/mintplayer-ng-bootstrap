Modals

bs-modal renders its dialog at the end of <body>, so no overflow: hidden ancestor clips it. [(open)] is two-way; Escape, the backdrop and the header's close button close it. The content is the body; the *bsModalHeader slot is the title that names the dialog, and the *bsModalFooter slot holds its actions. Any element inside with data-bs-dismiss="modal" closes it, as in Bootstrap.
