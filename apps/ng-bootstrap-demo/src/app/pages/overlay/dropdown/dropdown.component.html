Dropdown

The bsDropdown structural directive on an <ng-template> inside an element turns that element into a menu button. A click toggles the panel, rendered at the end of <body> so no overflow: hidden ancestor clips it; ArrowDown and ArrowUp open it on the first and the last item. Escape, a click outside, tabbing out or choosing an item closes it, and focus returns to the trigger. The directive's value is the placement — bottom-start (the default), bottom-end, top-start or top-end — and it flips vertically when there is no room.

Menu

bs-dropdown-menu is an ARIA menu over its bs-dropdown-items: the arrow keys, Home and End move between them. An item's content, or the link or button it wraps, is the menu item.

Any content

With hasPopup="dialog" the panel holds content that is not a menu — a calendar, a form — and takes focus itself.
